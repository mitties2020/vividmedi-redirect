# Clinical and service references

These sources informed the wording and structure of the demonstration. They should be reviewed again with the relevant WA Health service, lived-experience representatives, Aboriginal health/cultural leads, privacy, clinical governance and digital-health teams before any pilot.

## WA Mental Health Hospital in the Home

- [South Metropolitan Health Service — Mental Health Hospital in the Home](https://smhs.health.wa.gov.au/Our-services/Mental-health/Peel-and-Rockingham-Kwinana/Mental-Health-Hospital-in-the-Home)
  - Daily mental health care at home, recovery focus, collaboration with patients and consented family/carers, daily nursing visits, medical review, pharmacy/social-work involvement and telehealth.

## Safety planning

- [WA Health — Safety Planning for Mental Health Consumers Policy](https://www.health.wa.gov.au/About-us/Policy-frameworks/Mental-Health/Mandatory-requirements/Safety-Planning-for-Mental-Health-Consumers-Policy)
- [WA Health — Safety Planning Procedures for Mental Health Consumers](https://www.health.wa.gov.au/~/media/Corp/Policy-Frameworks/Mental-Health/Safety-Planning-for-Mental-Health-Consumers-Policy/Supporting/Safety-Planning-Procedures-for-Mental-Health-Consumers.pdf)
- [WA Health — Principles and Best Practice for the Care of People Who May Be Suicidal](https://www.health.wa.gov.au/~/media/Corp/Policy-Frameworks/Mental-Health/Safety-Planning-for-Mental-Health-Consumers-Policy/Supporting/Principles-and-Best-Practice-for-the-Care-of-People-Who-May-Be-Suicidal.pdf)
  - The prototype follows the themes of recovery-oriented, person-centred, trauma-informed and culturally competent care; collaborative planning; personal triggers and warning signs; strengths and goals; agreed responsibilities; crisis actions; consented involvement of support people; and regular review.

## WA urgent support

- [East Metropolitan Health Service — Mental Health Emergency Response Line](https://www.emhs.health.wa.gov.au/Hospitals-and-Services/Mental-Health-Alcohol-and-Other-Drugs/Inpatient-and-Other-Services/MHERL)
  - MHERL: Perth `1300 555 788`; Peel `1800 676 822`. MHERL is a 24-hour mental health crisis service and is not an emergency service. In an emergency call `000`.
- [HealthyWA — Mental health emergency](https://www.health.wa.gov.au/sitecore/content/Healthy-WA/Articles/J_M/Mental-health-emergency?sc_lang=en)
  - RuralLink: `1800 552 002`; Lifeline: `13 11 14`; Beyond Blue: `1300 22 4636`.

## Outcome measures

- [WA Community Mental Health Patient Activity Data Business Rules 2022](https://www.health.wa.gov.au/-/media/Corp/Policy-Frameworks/Information-Management/Patient%20Activity%20Data/Supporting/Community-Mental-Health-Patient-Activity-Data-Business-Rules-2022.pdf)
  - Lists HoNOS, LSP-16 and consumer-rated measures including K10 for adult community mental health reporting.
- [Australian Mental Health Outcomes and Classification Network — Consumer self-assessment](https://data.amhocn.org/dst/mhcose/)
  - Describes offering consumer self-assessment in public mental health services and the role of K10.
- [Australian Institute of Health and Welfare — K10 score](https://meteor.aihw.gov.au/content/376091)
  - K10 scoring and Australian range labels used in this demonstration.

PHQ-9, GAD-7 and WHO-5 are included as optional demonstration cards, not represented as WA-mandated measures. Their final inclusion, wording, licensing, scoring, cadence and clinical response protocol require local clinical-governance approval.
